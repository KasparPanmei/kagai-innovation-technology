1. Identity of Data Fiduciary & Operational Scope

This Statutory Policy governs the operations of KAGAI Innovation Technology Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as the "Company", "KAGAI", or the "Data Fiduciary" as codified in Section 2(i) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). KAGAI is incorporated with global registered seats in London (UK Company House Registration: 14982103) and India operations managed through KAGAI Innovation Technology Private Limited (CIN: U72900KA2023PTC174829), with headquarters situated at Bangalore Technology Hub, Outer Ring Road, Bellandur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560103.

Applicable Business Domains & Scopes:

  • Enterprise Consulting & Software: Architecture, microservices orchestration, custom ERP/CRM deployments, and multi-tenant cloud software.
  • IoT Hardware & Embedded Firmware: Industrial telemetry units, SCADA bridge controllers, and connected sensing fabrics deployed at enterprise facilities.

SECTION 05 • SOVEREIGNTY & TRANSIT

5. Cross-Border Sovereign Data Transfers

KAGAI rigorously observes the cross-border transfer strictures mandated under Section 16 of the DPDP Act 2023, along with European Union Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs 2021/914/EU) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA).

Statutory Transfer Guardrails:

  1. Negative List Observance: KAGAI maintains real-time regulatory compliance ensuring no digital personal data originating from India is routed to any foreign jurisdiction restricted by the Central Government of India under DPDP Section 16(1).
  2. Data Localization by Default: All Academy student records and domestic Indian enterprise client telemetry reside primarily within AWS ap-south-1 (Mumbai) and ap-south-2 (Hyderabad) datacenters.
  3. Zero Interception Transit: Cross-region operational data replication between the UK and India relies strictly on IPSec IKEv2 tunnels with PFS (Perfect Forward Secrecy).

SECTION 07 • STATUTORY DATA PRINCIPAL RIGHTS

7. Data Principal Rights & Redressal

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, data principals enjoy legally enforceable rights regarding their digital identity. KAGAI guarantees unconditional execution of the following:

visibility Right to Access Information (Section 11)

The principal may request an intelligible summary of personal data being processed, identity of all entities with whom data has been shared, and any supplemental operational insight.

edit_note Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12)

The principal may demand rectification of incomplete or misleading records, updating of outdated details, and systemic erasure of records no longer necessary for contracted purposes.

family_restroom Right of Nomination (Section 14)

Statutory entitlement to designate another natural individual who shall, in the event of death or incapacity of the principal, exercise the principal's privacy and data retrieval rights.
