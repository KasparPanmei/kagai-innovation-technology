SECTION 02.0 // CRITICAL MANDATE Strictly Enforced

Cybersecurity Testing & Authorized Scope Notice

gavel

Bilateral Rules of Engagement (RoE) Requirement

All offensive security diagnostics, red-teaming simulations, adversary emulation, dynamic fuzzing, and authorized penetration testing operations offered by KAGAI are conducted exclusively under mutually executed, legally binding Rules of Engagement (RoE) signed by corporate officers of legal capacity.

Explicit Prohibitions Against KAGAI Digital Infrastructure:
  • Automated Vulnerability Scanning: Running automated crawlers, Acunetix, Nessus, Burp Suite Enterprise, or continuous mass-scanners against *.kagai.com clusters without formal written white-listing is strictly prohibited.
  • Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS): Volumetric layer 3/4 flooding, distributed slowloris HTTP attacks, or state-exhaustion tests against KAGAI API gateways are treated as statutory computer misuse offenses under the Computer Misuse Act 1990 (UK) and the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA - 18 U.S.C. § 1030).
  • Social Engineering: Spear-phishing, credential harvesting, voice pretexting, or physical breach attempts targeting KAGAI personnel, research fellows, or facilities are strictly banned.

KAGAI supports independent security researchers acting in good faith. If you identify a zero-day or configuration vulnerability in our public-facing scope, report it in strict adherence to our Responsible Disclosure Policy. We will not pursue civil litigation or recommend criminal referral against security researchers who: (1) give us reasonable time to remediate prior to public disclosure, (2) make no attempt to exfiltrate proprietary customer data, and (3) execute zero persistent modifications to runtime memory or persistent storage.
