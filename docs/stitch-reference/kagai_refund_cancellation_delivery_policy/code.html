Strictly Non-Refundable Components

Certain specialized elements represent consumed intellectual property, transit costs, or dedicated physical silicon hardware which cannot be refunded once dispatched or administered:

  • Physical Evaluation Hardware: ARM Cortex, RISC-V, and FPGA developer kits once tamper-evident transit seals are broken.
  • Defensible Capstone Examination Fees: Third-party proctoring allocations and academic assessment panel stipends.
  • Dedicated Sandbox Instances: Dedicated AWS/GCP GPU cluster compute allocated exclusively to fellowship accounts.
Hardware kits returned in unopened, original factory-sealed condition within 14 days incur a standard 15% restocking fee.

Student Deferral Privileges

Enrolled fellows encounter unexpected corporate milestones or personal emergencies. KAGAI grants every fellow a unilateral, no-penalty one-time cohort deferral.

Qualifying Criteria: • Formal notice submitted prior to Week 4 live lab session. • Medical certification or signed enterprise supervisor note. • Guaranteed placement in any term starting within 180 days.
Subsequent secondary deferrals incur an administrative rescheduling levy of $250 USD.
