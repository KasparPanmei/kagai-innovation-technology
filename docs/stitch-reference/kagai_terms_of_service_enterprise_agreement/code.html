gavel Preamble & Parties

This Master Platform Agreement and Terms of Service (hereinafter, the "Agreement") is entered into by and between KAGAI Innovation Technology Ltd. (together with its worldwide affiliates, "KAGAI", "we", "us", or "our") and the corporate entity, institutional partner, or individual accessing KAGAI digital platforms, APIs, hardware firmware images, or training environments ("Client", "Licensee", or "User"). By accessing or utilizing any KAGAI service, the Client represents that its representative has full corporate authority to bind the entity.

01.0

Acceptance of Terms & Capacity

1.1 Capacity and Authority: The services provided by KAGAI are exclusively intended for commercial, institutional, and professional engineering deployment. You warrant that you are of legal age and possess full corporate capacity to execute contracts in the jurisdiction where your operations are situated.

1.2 Ecosystem Scope: This Agreement governs all interfaces operated under the KAGAI technical umbrella, including but not limited to:

  • The KAGAI Public Portal, Developer Hub, and REST/gRPC API telemetry pipelines.
  • Enterprise Cloud Management Portals and Micro-services Orchestration clusters.
  • Embedded cryptographic firmware binaries distributed for Industrial IoT controllers.
  • The KAGAI Academy learning infrastructure, code repos, and interactive labs.

1.3 Academy Participants: Individuals registered under the KAGAI Academy cohort programs are additionally bound by academic integrity standards, intellectual property non-disclosure rules, and compute sandbox quotas specified in Section 6.

02.0

Enterprise Engagements & Master Service Schedules

2.1 Statements of Work (SOW): Commercial technology advisory, bespoke software architecture, and firmware prototyping shall be documented in individually numbered Statements of Work executed by authorized signatories.

2.2 Order of Precedence: In the event of a direct conflict between the terms of this Agreement and any executed SOW, the specific terms of the SOW shall supersede solely for the scope, milestones, and deliverables defined in that particular schedule.

03.0

Intellectual Property & Bespoke Code Ownership

3.1 Client Deliverables Assignment: Conditioned upon full and final payment of all corresponding milestone fees, KAGAI hereby assigns and transfers to the Client all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to bespoke application source code and domain models specifically authored for the Client under an executed SOW ("Client Deliverables").

3.2 KAGAI Background IP & Microkernels: Notwithstanding Section 3.1, KAGAI retains sole and exclusive ownership of all pre-existing software, base microkernel code, firmware drivers, distributed consensus libraries, mathematical schemas, automated deployment pipelines, and pedagogical assets authored independently of the SOW ("KAGAI Core IP").

04.0

Software Licensing & Edge Firmware Restrictions

4.1 Firmware Execution Rights: KAGAI grants to the authorized enterprise partner a revocable, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license to flash, run, and execute compiled firmware binary images exclusively on certified edge devices deployed within approved operational perimeters.

warning
Absolute Prohibitions on Binary Modification

Licensee shall not, directly or through third-party agents: (i) decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer, or decrypt firmware binary blobs; (ii) bypass or attempt to manipulate Hardware Security Modules (HSM) or cryptographic roots of trust; or (iii) bridge air-gapped test images to unsecured wide-area public networks.

4.2 Automated Integrity Auditing: KAGAI edge runtimes embed cryptographic attestation tokens. In the event of unauthorized modification or hash mismatches, the runtime may automatically lock down secure partitions to prevent systemic operational compromise.

05.0

Service Levels & Cloud Telemetry (SLA)

5.1 Availability Commitment: KAGAI guarantees a 99.995% Service Level Agreement ("SLA") for dedicated production-grade cloud telemetry endpoints, excluding pre-notified scheduled maintenance windows.

Target Uptime 99.995% Multi-region active failover
P1 Response Time < 15 mins 24x7x365 direct SOC bridge
Service Credit Cap 30.0% Of applicable monthly billing

5.2 Service Credit Escalation: In the event uptime falls below agreed thresholds during any calendar month, Client shall be entitled to credits in accordance with the executed Service Level Addendum. Service credits represent Client's sole and exclusive remedy for availability impairments.

06.0

Acceptable Use & Security Guardrails

6.1 Security Integrity Requirements: Users and partner organizations shall not engage in actions that degrade, compromise, or monitor unauthorized production clusters.

block Penetration Testing Restrictions

Penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, and red-team drills are strictly prohibited without a mutually signed Security Clearance Addendum issued at least ten (10) business days prior to test initiation.

speed Rate Limiting & Telemetry Floods

API traffic is governed by configured throughput quotas. Artificial load spikes, denial-of-service simulations, and unthrottled loop ingestion will trigger automatic IP blacklisting and immediate circuit interruption.

6.2 KAGAI Academy Conduct: Students utilizing Academy lab clusters must not run cryptomining scripts, network scanners, or malicious payloads against neighboring student sandbox tenants. Non-compliance results in immediate revocation of credentials without tuition reimbursement.

07.0

Limitation of Liability & Consequential Damages

TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, LOSS OF REVENUE, LOSS OF BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY, CORRUPTION OF DATA, OR PRODUCTION DOWNTIME, REGARDLESS OF THE LEGAL THEORY ASSERTED.

7.1 Aggregate Liability Cap: In no event shall KAGAI's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence), or otherwise, exceed the aggregate sums actually paid by the Client to KAGAI under the applicable Statement of Work during the twelve (12) calendar months preceding the incident giving rise to liability.

7.2 Exclusions: The limitations specified in this Section 7 shall not apply to: (i) gross negligence or willful misconduct; (ii) breaches of Section 4 (Firmware & Software Licensing Restrictions); or (iii) indemnification obligations for third-party intellectual property claims.

08.0

Governing Law, Arbitration & Dispute Resolution

8.1 Escalation and Negotiation: The parties agree to resolve any dispute, controversy, or claim arising under or relating to this Agreement first through good-faith executive escalation. Within ten (10) business days of written notice, designated senior executives of each party shall convene to attempt an amicable commercial resolution.

Forum A: Asia-Pacific & India Ops

For engagements originated or performed within India or the APAC theater: Administered under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. Proceedings in English before a sole arbitrator.

Forum B: UK, Europe & Global Ops

For engagements originated or performed within EMEA, the Americas, or international waters: Governed by the laws of England & Wales. Settled under the Rules of the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA). The seat shall be London, UK.

8.2 Injunctive Relief: Nothing herein prevents either party from seeking preliminary or emergency injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction to protect its trade secrets, intellectual property, or confidential systems.
